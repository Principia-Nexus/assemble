## Twenty capability contracts

The numbered contracts correspond to research reports 11–30. Each uses the shared permission, version and recovery rules.

11. **Readability.** Inspect supported inputs by purpose. Record immutable source hash/version, PDF page versus printed sheet, region, row/column and method: text, OCR, visual or user clarification. Preserve transformations and attempt outcomes. Distinguish readable, partial, uninspected, unreadable, unsupported, unavailable and conflicting. Validate manifest/page coverage. Table values require headings, units and footnotes. Never report whole-set review from sampled pages or treat empty extraction as blank content.

12. **Source authority.** Register issuer, project/site, stated date/revision, received time, issue purpose and applicability evidence separately. Upload order, “FINAL,” a fingerprint or a newer-looking file establishes no authority. Apply precedence only within an evidenced governing clause and resolved scope. Preserve complete replacement instructions and ambiguous targets; do not merge superseded clauses silently. A stable URL can serve changed bytes.

13. **Cross-references.** Retain literal identifiers, punctuation, suffixes and leading zeros, with entity kind and project/building/system namespace. Resolve compatible endpoints and revisions; normalization only proposes matches. Track explicit, inferred and user-asserted relations as resolved, ambiguous, missing or unreadable. A reference proves neither installation nor sequence. Duplicate identifiers and detached table headers block the affected association, not unrelated work.

14. **Scope structure.** Extract obligations separately from presentation groups, responsibility packages, tasks, deliverables and milestones. Preserve furnish/install/connect/program/test/train/closeout distinctions and exclusions. Allow private create, split, merge and regroup with undo. Every obligation survives restructuring with a destination or visible unresolved disposition. Specification divisions do not automatically assign firms; containment does not establish sequence. Reorganization never transfers consent.

15. **Roles and qualifications.** Record proposed roles separately from evidenced appointments, with scope, jurisdiction, date and review purpose. Credential research, when supported and requested, returns official-source findings and identity/type limitations; no “qualified” badge. Project owner, firm representative, EOR, designer, coordinator, performer and witness are distinct roles. Software access, license matches and commercial responses confer no professional authority.

16. **Quote coverage.** Map requirements and activities to quoted inclusions, exclusions, conditions, unstated items, conflicts or unreadable clauses. Preserve negation despite “complete installation” headlines. Compare only explicit common scope and price bases; retain allowances, alternate dependencies and mutual exclusions. Missing requirements mean project completeness unassessed. Potential overlap is not an automatic credit, and the cheapest stated price proves no suitability.

17. **Quantities and units.** Preserve raw value/unit, quantity kind, precision, basis, location, headers and qualifiers. Distinguish type properties from installed instances and supplied estimates from verified quantities. Compute only from supported operands and compatible units; retain conversions and rounding. Never count OCR hits or schedule types as inventory, measure engineering dimensions from pixels, invent waste factors, or treat blanks/dashes as zero. Prevent duplicate detail/subtotal aggregation.

18. **Optional schedules.** Propose tentative private activities, durations and dependencies with stated assumptions. Software calculates supported date/calendar semantics; distinguish target, deadline, quoted availability, estimate and computed finish. Require anchors, working/elapsed units, calendar exceptions and supported dependency/lag types. Reject cycles and unsupported conversions; retain unscheduled work. Review, procurement and testing remain explicit activities where relevant. A duration never supplies approval or resource feasibility.

19. **Capacity.** Keep capability claims, dated availability, quantitative resource limits and exact-term consent separate. One firm need not equal one crew. Check compatible workload/pool units, intervals, validity, conditions, gross/net availability and overlapping allocations; avoid counting both a pool and its constituents or subtracting outside commitments twice. Unknown outside obligations stay unknown. Unsupported models remain unassessed; retain the existing crew-week checker only for its declared assumptions.

20. **Commercial arithmetic.** Calculate supplied prices with decimal money and explicit currency, tax, allowances, alternates, rounding and inclusion bases. Reconcile extensions against quoted totals without silently choosing. Missing tax, FX, quantity or fees remain unresolved. Preserve payer/payee, payment denominator, trigger, retainage, advance minimum and validity. Separate supported subtotal from complete proposed total. Budget headroom is neither profit nor available cash; no automatic payment or alternate exercise.

21. **Questions and conditions.** Create purpose-specific questions for read failures, missing references, ambiguity, conflicts or unassigned responsibility. Record affected conclusions, resolution criteria, responsible responder and evidence. Ask the smallest consequential question, deduplicate shared causes and accept “unknown” without a questionnaire loop. An answer is not necessarily resolution; acknowledgments cannot replace missing evidence. RFI or clarification drafts remain unsent.

22. **Reviews and acceptance.** Separate firm commercial responses, technical dispositions, test records and customer quote acceptance. Bind each to exact subjects, terms, dependencies, purpose, actor, authority, time, conditions and validity. Prepare a customer-ready quote with supported scope/exclusions, price basis and material commercial terms; disclose unresolved conditions. Only the responsible human selects/shares it, and the authorized customer explicitly accepts that version. Offer expiry blocks new acceptance of an unaccepted offer; it does not expire or terminate an already accepted customer agreement. Chat assent, delivery, partner consent and imported claims never substitute. Acceptance is not engineering approval or permission to mobilize. Technical dispositions remain evidence tracking unless a purpose-specific authorized workflow is implemented; AI never supplies professional approval.

23. **Change impact.** Compare immutable baseline/input versions and trace supported dependencies within a finite bound. Return located before/after changes, proposed edits and stale checks. For firm responses, preserve existing validity/withdrawal rules and explain unchanged-valid, fresh-response-required, needs-assessment, expired or withdrawn states. Missing links mean incomplete impact assessment. Preview/discard preserves valid baselines and incoming requests. Source changes or private proposals flag impact and prepare applicable firm review or a customer amendment; they never rewrite or rescind the accepted customer snapshot. Unrelated changes do not invalidate everything. Undo never revives withdrawn, expired or invalidated firm responses.

24. **Interfaces and handoffs.** Record producer/consumer scope, required artifact revision, deliverable, prerequisites and review purpose. Separate supply, installation, power/control connections, programming, testing, witnessing and review. Shared activities need explicit boundaries. Information flow establishes neither subcontracting nor scheduling. Compare source responsibilities with quote exclusions; missing interface ownership becomes a question, not an automatic assignment.

25. **Workspace composition.** Select the few fixed components that answer the request: scope/group, task, milestone, comparison, bounded run, evidence, question or review. Software owns identities/layout; no generated HTML or duplicate AI plan. Graph/List/Week/Report project identical permitted records. View switching runs no AI. Keep undated work and material unresolved-condition summaries visible under grouping/filtering. Offer precise manual edits, Undo and Show changes.

26. **Typed tools.** Questions receive read-only tools; authorized private edits use allowlisted commands shared with manual edits. Selecting terms, external sharing, sending and human responses use separate deliberate endpoints unavailable to AI. Validate semantic support, schema, permissions, transitions, units, edges and version preconditions server-side. Atomically commit; bind idempotency to actor/project/action/payload. Return committed/rejected/unknown receipts, never model-asserted success. No arbitrary SQL, code or unrestricted connectors.

27. **Runs and recovery.** Persist queued/working/needs-input/draft-ready/failed/stopped state. Initial configurable tuning limits: four model calls, twelve tools, 120 seconds, 48,000 cumulative input and 6,000 output tokens, including one repair and retries. Stop sooner on cancellation or repeated no progress. Refresh reads the same run; uncertain commits reconcile before retry. One mutation run per private draft; reject stale output and retain input. Cancellation revokes future writes, while earlier commits remain visible.

28. **Privacy.** Enforce source audience and processing purpose before retrieval, external processing, rendering and commit. Reads, edits, downloads, exports, AI processing, sharing and responses have separate rights. Derivatives/caches inherit restrictions; customer publication requires deliberate review and an authorized audience grant. Never leak peer prices, hidden names/counts or internal costs through summaries. Revoke pending access and derived caches when grants change. Disable private session replay/body logging; retain sanitized operational metadata and explicit deletion coverage.

29. **Untrusted inputs.** Documents, OCR, images, retrieved text and summaries remain data. “Submit drawings” can become a requirement, never execution. Embedded instructions cannot alter prices, permissions or tools. Preserve suspect originals; extract legitimate neighboring content without consequential tools. Never execute macros/scripts or auto-load links/images. If acquisition is enabled, authorize destinations, validate MIME/bytes and bounded parsing, reject credential-bearing URLs, and block redirects/DNS/private-network/metadata bypasses. No unapproved provider fallback or private web-search terms.

30. **Evaluation.** Freeze original inputs, parser/media inputs, permitted actors, expected facts, required unknowns, allowed deltas and forbidden effects. Keep independent answer keys outside runtime retrieval. Reinspect synthetic mutations; blurring an image may leave readable embedded text. Evaluate facts, abstentions, permission/state integrity and actual saved outcomes, not polished prose. Repeat after parser, retrieval, model, schema, pack or checker changes.

## Ten contextual profiles

These profiles suggest extraction and questions; their references are not automatically adopted project requirements.

1. **Requirements/BOD:** connect owner criteria, design assumptions, artifacts and issues; preserve issuer, phase and revision. [USGBC commissioning guidance](https://www.usgbc.org/node/2769851) is program-specific; rating-system applicability must be established.
2. **Drawing sets:** retain indexes, legends, revisions, keyed notes and schedule/sequence links. [SCC Addendum 1](https://www.sccsc.edu/media/spartanburgcc/content-assets/about/purchasing--operations/construction-solicitations/H59-N385-PD_Addendum-1-rev.pdf) illustrates mechanical/electrical cross-references; inspection covered selected base sheets and amendment regions.
3. **Architecture:** distinguish rooms, openings, finishes, demolition/retention and new work. [USC Aiken drawings](https://sc.edu/purchasing/solicitations/documents/20084_USCA%20AHSS_Issued%20for%20Bidding_2021_0129%20Rev%201-drwg.pdf) were inspected selectively in text; nominal door width establishes no accessibility verdict.
4. **Structure:** preserve criteria, loads/units, equipment interfaces, delegated scope, geotechnical references and inspection requirements. [ECC drawings](https://www.eastcentral.edu/common/bid-opportunities/ECC-Rolla-USGS-Renovations-CD-Drawings.pdf), selected S-001/S-002, contain unresolved criteria; never infer spare capacity or size members.
5. **Mechanical/controls:** link equipment, sequences, points and furnish/install/connect/program/TAB/training responsibilities. [Clark County controls ODR](https://assets.ctfassets.net/2pnrhs9eukyp/KAZiGMsMKdyRzw9D5JlvL/76d83dbb8489d87e5497a1e449a76c1c/23_09_23_-_DIRECT_DIGITAL_CONTROL_FOR_HVAC__v0_.pdf) requires project integration; missing sequences remain missing.
6. **Electrical:** separate demolition/new risers, fixture types, room controls, circuits, temporary utilities and outage conditions. [MHSD Addendum D](https://www.mhsdla.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/ELECTRICAL_Addendum-D.pdf), E120/E130, demonstrates spatially separate evidence; no inferred counts, connections or adequacy.
7. **Plumbing:** link fixtures, service types, schedules, risers, tie-ins, field wiring and test/report requirements. [VA plumbing manual](https://www.cfm.va.gov/til/dManual/dmPlbg.pdf) is agency guidance, not installed inventory; existing flow, pressure and connection condition need evidence.
8. **Fire/life safety:** separate design allocation, interfaces, preliminary tests, witnesses and acceptance. [UFGS 28 31 76](https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/DOD/UFGS/UFGS%2028%2031%2076.pdf) contains template alternatives; do not invent adopted editions, AHJ approval or system adequacy.
9. **Civil/site:** preserve datum, epoch, units, surveyed extent, utility evidence, borings and dated groundwater observations. [FHWA SUE guidance](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/programadmin/sueindex.cfm) distinguishes evidence levels; a local exposure proves no continuous underground route or excavation safety.
10. **Specifications/procurement:** separate work results, contractual packages, quote coverage and assignments; track alternates, allowances and replacements. [Brownsville Addendum 5](https://www.portofbrownsville.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Addendum-5-PA-8-Dike-Relocation.pdf) includes an obsolete calendar and conflicting sheet identifiers; preserve both conflicts.

## Concrete reading failures

Use an initial supported method, one alternate and one contextual crop/orientation retry within run limits. Large sets use manifest-backed chunks; skipped pages remain uninspected. Unsupported CAD/BIM, encrypted or unavailable sources receive honest status.

- “E130's panel rows lost column alignment. Paste the panel name, relevant row, headings and units in chat.” Retain readable notes; withhold dependent circuit conclusions.
- “The AHU sequence page is blurred. Paste its operating sequence and revision.” Preserve equipment scope and controls/TAB exclusions; do not invent points.
- “The grading datum is readable, but the utility elevation is not. Type the elevation and its datum.” Keep the partial inventory; withhold clearance.

Use actual known locators. User replies retain actor/time attribution; they never become recovered drawing extraction, firm consent or professional approval. Distinguish parser failure, absent content and unknown set completeness. Stop only dependent conclusions.

## Verification and provenance

Run three isolated trials per frozen case. Require every critical provenance, authority, abstention and state assertion to pass every trial. Initial proposed semantic gates are ≥95% factual precision and ≥90% required-readable-fact coverage per applicable case; report counts, unnecessary abstentions, processing coverage and worst trial. These thresholds do not establish population reliability.

Include readable/blurred/truncated inputs, missing references, revision conflicts, template alternatives, negation/unit changes, one firm without dates, scope exclusions, forged consent, injection, wrong-project access, revocation mid-run, stale/concurrent writes, duplicate retries, cancellation and reload. Verify view parity and exact-version customer acceptance, including a changed quote while its acceptance page is open. An expired unaccepted offer rejects new acceptance; earlier customer acceptance survives the offer-expiry date, source changes and private amendment drafting. Retain canonical twelve-site $168,000/$172,000 regression and affected-response rules. No automatic external effect is permitted.

Record build/origin, actual model/adapter/media route, pack/schema/checker versions, source/base revisions, expected/observed deltas and sanitized receipts as PASS/FAIL/NOT RUN/BLOCKED. Human-check critical labels independently; unresolved annotation is BLOCKED.
